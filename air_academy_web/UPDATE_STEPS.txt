=======================================================================================

WHAT YOU GET
- THE POLICY PAGES that Paystack and Visa/Mastercard check before they approve card payments:
  Terms of Service (/terms), Refund Policy (/refund-policy), Delivery Policy (/delivery-policy) and
  Privacy Policy (/privacy-policy), plus a business footer at the bottom of every page, including the
  home page. Your own name and street address are NOT shown anywhere (see "THE POLICY PAGES" below).
- A tick box at sign-up ("I agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy") and a short agreement line with links
  under every Unlock button.
- A WELCOME VIDEO plays full screen the moment the app opens. When it ends, an EXPLORE button pops
  up and the student taps it to continue (see "WELCOME VIDEO" below).
- Payments run on PAYSTACK only. Flutterwave and Stripe are gone from the app.
- Paystack needs ONE key on Render. No webhook password, no public key.
- A student taps Unlock, pays on Paystack's secure page (card, bank transfer or USSD), comes back
  to your site, and the course opens by itself.
- A green "N live - Control Room" button on YOUR dashboard only. It shows how many people are
  using the app right now (one person = 1, two = 2, and so on), refreshed every few seconds.
- The Control Room (only your email can open it): live users, an OpenAI/Resend/Supabase/Render
  cost meter with warnings and email alerts, this month's bill with Paystack's fees counted,
  a "what if I had 1,000 students" slider, sales, sign-ups, a launch checklist, and a tool to give
  a student a track.
- It warns you (red) if a Paystack TEST key is left on your live site.
- Your students' accounts, courses and payments are NOT touched. Old payments stay counted as
  sales. New tables are added by themselves when the app starts.

STEP 1. COPY THE FILES (PowerShell, inside the folder that has app.py)

    Expand-Archive -Path "$HOME\Downloads\air_academy_paystack_legal_pages.zip" -DestinationPath . -Force

README.md and the static files. It does not touch your .env, your database or your lesson files.
(Use THIS zip, not the earlier ones. It contains everything.)

STEP 2. RENDER SETTINGS (Dashboard > your service > Environment)

    OWNER_EMAILS       = your-own-gmail-address@gmail.com   (the Gmail you sign in with)
    PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY = sk_live_...
    SUPPORT_EMAIL      = a-new-support-address@gmail.com     (NEW, needed: see "THE POLICY PAGES")

Optional, only if you want them shown on the website (leave them out to keep them private):
    SUPPORT_PHONE      = a phone number for customers
    BUSINESS_LOCATION  = a town or city only, for example Lagos (without it the site says "Based in Nigeria")
    BUSINESS_ADDRESS   = a street address (not shown unless you set this)
    BUSINESS_REG_NO    = your CAC registration number, if you have one
    BUSINESS_NAME      = only if the public name should be different from "Open Air Tech Space"

Type your real address in OWNER_EMAILS on Render only. It is deliberately not in the code or in
this file, because your code is on public GitHub.

Where the Paystack key is: Paystack dashboard > Settings > API Keys & Webhooks. Copy the
SECRET key (it starts with sk_live_). Do NOT use the public key (starts with pk_live_): it cannot
confirm payments, and the Control Room will tell you if you paste it by mistake.
Never send the secret key to anybody, not even to me.

Delete these from Render if they exist (the app no longer reads them):
    FLW_PUBLIC_KEY, FLW_SECRET_KEY, FLW_WEBHOOK_HASH, FLW_USD_AMOUNT, and anything starting STRIPE_

Also check:
  - APP_ENV=production
  - DEMO_UNLOCK must NOT exist

Settings > Start Command (so one slow OpenAI answer does not freeze everybody else). Your repository
has a folder called air_academy_web, so the command is:

    gunicorn --chdir air_academy_web app:app --workers 2 --threads 4 --timeout 120

STEP 3. PAYSTACK DASHBOARD (two small things)

       https://YOUR-SITE.onrender.com/api/paystack/webhook

   request it. Paystack reviews it, usually within a couple of working days. After it is approved,
   a student abroad pays the same N4,000 with their own card and their bank converts the money.
   Until it is approved, foreign cards are declined. Nothing is needed in the app for this.
   (A separate dollar price is not part of this update. It would need a US dollar account with
   Paystack's partner bank.)

THE POLICY PAGES (for Paystack and the card companies)
Why they exist: before Paystack turns on live card payments, they and the card companies (Visa, Mastercard)
look at your website for: your business name, a way for customers to contact you, the price and what is sold,
a refund policy, a delivery policy, a privacy policy and terms. All of that is now on your site.

Your privacy: the pages show only "Open Air Tech Space", "Based in Nigeria" and the support email you set.
Your name and street address are not in the app, the code or this zip. Visa and Mastercard ask for a business
name and ONE way to contact you (email, phone or address), not all three.
- Make a NEW free email for the app (for example openairtechspace.support@gmail.com) and put it in
  SUPPORT_EMAIL. Do not use the Gmail you sign in with: it is shown to everybody, and the Control Room
  turns yellow if you do.
- Your real name and address still go in Paystack's own business form (Settings > Business / Go Live). That
  form is private and only Paystack sees it. Use the same details as your registration documents.
- If a reviewer ever asks for an address on the site, set BUSINESS_ADDRESS on Render and it appears at once.

Promises the policies make (change the numbers at the top of legal.py if you want different ones, then push):
    refund for a change of mind: 7 days after payment, if no final exam taken and no certificate yet
    we answer emails within 2 business days; a refund is started within 3 business days of approval
    a course that did not open is fixed within 1 business day, or refunded
    data requests (see, fix or delete data) answered within 30 days
    30 days' notice before a paid course is ever withdrawn
ONLY KEEP PROMISES YOU WILL REALLY KEEP. Also change UPDATED at the top of legal.py to the new date.

Things the app does not do by itself, that the policies describe as done by request (by email):
- Refunds: you press refund in the Paystack dashboard. The student's access to that course is not closed
  automatically yet. If you want that automated, ask me.
- Deleting an account: there is no delete button yet. You do it on request.

These pages are a solid, honest starting point written for how your app really works. They are not legal

PAYSTACK FEES (so there are no surprises)
Paystack keeps about 1.5% plus N100 from a local card payment. On a N4,000 course that is about
N160, so you receive about N3,840. Foreign cards cost more (about 3.9% plus N100). The Control Room
estimates these and you can correct the rates under Plans. Your Paystack dashboard shows the exact fee.

WELCOME VIDEO
What a student sees: the video starts by itself the moment the app opens, full screen. When it ends,
an EXPLORE button pops up; tapping it opens the app. There is a "Skip" button from the first second.
- Sound: phones and browsers do not let sound start by itself in most cases, so the video usually
  plays silent with a "Tap for sound" button. (In the installed app it may start with sound.)
- It plays once each time the app is opened, not on every page. To change that, edit SHOW at the top
  of static/intro.js ("session", "day" or "always").
- It steps aside by itself (the student goes straight to the app) when the video is missing or cannot
  play, when it has not started after 7 seconds (slow data), when Data Saver or a 2G connection is on,
  when the phone is set to reduce motion, and when a student comes back from paying.
- To look at the app without the video, open your site with /?nointro at the end of the address.
- It is about 0.9 MB. Phones keep it for a week, so it is downloaded once.

TO REPLACE THE VIDEO LATER
1. Make the new file H.264 video with AAC sound, about 2 MB or less and 10 seconds or less, and name
   it intro.mp4. Put it in the static folder, replacing the old one.
2. Open static/intro.js and add 1 to the number after "var VERSION =". This makes phones that already
   saved the old video download the new one.
3. Push as in STEP 4.
A VERTICAL (9:16) video fills a phone screen. A wide video (like the current one) shows as a wide band
across the middle of a phone, with dark space above and below. That still looks fine, but a vertical
version of the same video would look bigger on phones.
If your new video is too big, this PowerShell command makes a small copy (needs ffmpeg installed):
    ffmpeg -i mynewvideo.mp4 -vf "scale=-2:720" -c:v libx264 -crf 28 -preset slow -c:a aac -b:a 96k -movflags +faststart intro.mp4

ABOUT THE PIXVERSE MARK
The current intro.mp4 has PixVerse's free-plan watermark in its top-right corner. PixVerse offers
watermark-free export on its paid plans, and a paid course app should use a clean copy. When you have
one, replace the file as described above. Nothing else needs to change.

HOW THE LIVE COUNT WORKS
Every open tab pings the server every 30 seconds. Someone counts for 75 seconds after their last
ping. A hidden tab or a phone put down drops off within about a minute. You are not counted (so you
do not inflate it). To test it, open the app in a private window WITHOUT signing in: you appear as
a visitor. Or tap "Count me too" in the Live section.

ABOUT THE COST NUMBERS
They are estimates. OpenAI is counted from tokens at the public price list, so compare with
platform.openai.com now and then. Render, Supabase and Resend use the plans you enter. Paystack's
fee is estimated from the rates under Plans.
The "what if" slider assumes about 30 cents of AI per active student a month until you have 10
active students; after that it uses what your real students actually cost.

REFUNDS
Refund in the Paystack dashboard. The app does not take the track back automatically, so tell the
student, and ask me if you want that automated.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
- No green button: the OWNER_EMAILS value must match the email you sign in with exactly (capitals
  do not matter). Render restarts after you change it.
- "A Paystack payment had a problem": the student paid but something did not match (amount,
  currency or email), or Paystack refused to start a payment (usually a wrong key). The warning
  says which. If a student really paid, check it in your Paystack dashboard and use "Give a track"
  in the Control Room.
- "Students cannot pay": PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY is missing, or it is the public key (pk_...). Use the
  secret key (sk_live_...).
- A student paid, came back, and the course did not open: wait a minute and refresh. The webhook
  finishes it. If the course is still locked after 5 minutes, check Setup check for the webhook tick.
- A foreign card is declined: international payments are not approved yet in Paystack (Step 3B).
- Payments that are already in your database still count as revenue.
