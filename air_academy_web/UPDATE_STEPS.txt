STEP 1. COPY THE FILES (PowerShell)
Open PowerShell inside your project folder (the one that has app.py), then run:

    Expand-Archive -Path "$HOME\Downloads\air_academy_animation_tracks.zip" -DestinationPath . -Force

(Change the path if the zip is not in Downloads.)
This replaces app.py, omega.py, courses_seed.py, course_content.py, README.md and the static
folder, and adds 3 new lesson files. It does not touch your .env or your database.

STEP 2. CHECK ON YOUR COMPUTER (optional but recommended)
Add this line to your local .env file (NOT on Render):

    DEMO_UNLOCK=1

Then run:   python app.py
Open http://127.0.0.1:5000 , sign in, and you will see a "Demo Unlock Pro" button.
It opens every course on your computer only, so you can look at all the paid screens.
It never saves anything and it cannot work on Render.

STEP 3. PUT IT ON RENDER
    git add .
    git commit -m "Add 2D, 3D and AI animation tracks, free lessons and paywall"
    git push

Render rebuilds by itself. The 3 new courses are added to your database automatically when the
app starts. Do NOT add DEMO_UNLOCK on Render. Keep APP_ENV=production there.

IF YOU CHANGE THE FREE LESSONS OR PRICE LATER
Both are at the top of app.py:
    PRICE_NGN = 4000
    FREE_LESSONS = {"prompt-engineering": 2}
(Also change `const PRICE = 4000;` at the top of static/app.js so the screen shows the same number.)
Payments of 3,500 that were already started before the update are still accepted.
