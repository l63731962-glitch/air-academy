AIR ACADEMY: LIVE USERS + CONTROL ROOM UPDATE (payments stay on FLUTTERWAVE)
============================================================================

WHAT YOU GET
- Payments stay on Flutterwave. Stripe is switched OFF and stays off unless you add a Stripe key,
  which you will not do. Do not add any variable that starts with STRIPE_.
- A green "N live - Control Room" button on YOUR dashboard only. It shows how many people are
  using the app right now (one person = 1, two = 2, and so on), refreshed every few seconds.
- The Control Room (only your email can open it): live users, an OpenAI/Resend/Supabase/Render
  cost meter with warnings and email alerts, this month's bill with Flutterwave's fees counted,
  a "what if I had 1,000 students" slider, sales, sign-ups, a launch checklist, and a tool to give
  a student a track.
- It warns you (red) if Flutterwave TEST keys are left on your live site. Test keys would let
  anyone "pay" with Flutterwave's test card and get a track free.
- OPTIONAL: a dollar price for students outside Nigeria (see STEP 3).
- Your students' accounts, courses and payments are NOT touched. New tables are added by
  themselves when the app starts.

STEP 1. COPY THE FILES (PowerShell, inside the folder that has app.py)

    Expand-Archive -Path "$HOME\Downloads\air_academy_flutterwave_control_room.zip" -DestinationPath . -Force

This adds billing.py, control.py, static/live.js, static/owner.js and replaces app.py, omega.py,
README.md and the static files. It does not touch your .env, your database or your lesson files.
(Use THIS zip, not the earlier Stripe one. It contains everything.)

STEP 2. RENDER SETTINGS (Dashboard > your service > Environment)

    OWNER_EMAILS = your-own-gmail-address@gmail.com   (the Gmail you sign in with)

Type your real address here on Render only. It is deliberately not in the code or in this file,
because your code is on public GitHub.

Your Flutterwave keys (Flutterwave dashboard > Settings > API keys):

    FLW_PUBLIC_KEY   = FLWPUBK-...      (LIVE key: no "_TEST" in it)
    FLW_SECRET_KEY   = FLWSECK-...      (LIVE key: no "_TEST" in it)
    FLW_WEBHOOK_HASH = any long random password you make up (letters and numbers)

Use the pair from the same mode. A test key and a live key together will not work.

Also check:
  - APP_ENV=production
  - DEMO_UNLOCK must NOT exist
  - If you added any STRIPE_ variable earlier (STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, STRIPE_CURRENCY, STRIPE_AMOUNT,
    STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET), DELETE them. With no Stripe key the app uses Flutterwave.

Settings > Start Command (so one slow OpenAI answer does not freeze everybody else). Your repository
has a folder called air_academy_web, so the command is:

    gunicorn --chdir air_academy_web app:app --workers 2 --threads 4 --timeout 120

STEP 3. OPTIONAL: LET STUDENTS OUTSIDE NIGERIA PAY IN DOLLARS
Add on Render:

    FLW_USD_AMOUNT = 3        (a whole number of dollars; pick your own price)

Then every locked course shows a second button under the naira button:
"Paying from outside Nigeria? Pay $3 by international card". Dollar payments are card only
(Flutterwave offers bank transfer and USSD for naira only). Leave FLW_USD_AMOUNT out and the app
is naira only, exactly as before.
Before you turn it on, check two things with Flutterwave (dashboard or support chat):
  1. Your account is allowed to accept payments in US dollars.
  2. How and when your dollar sales are paid out to you (in dollars or in naira).
Flutterwave charges more for international cards (often up to about 3.8%). The Control Room
estimates it and you can correct the rate under Plans. Do one real test purchase with an
international card before you announce it.

STEP 4. FLUTTERWAVE WEBHOOK (so a payment unlocks even if the student closes the page)
Flutterwave dashboard > Settings > Webhooks:
  - URL:         https://YOUR-SITE.onrender.com/api/flutterwave/webhook
  - Secret hash: exactly the same value you put in FLW_WEBHOOK_HASH on Render

STEP 5. PUSH

    git add .
    git commit -m "Live users, Control Room and optional dollar price"
    git push

Render rebuilds. Sign in with your Gmail: you will see the green Control Room button.

STEP 6. OPEN THE CONTROL ROOM > SETUP CHECK, THEN PLANS
Setup check should show green ticks for "Flutterwave keys (live mode)", the webhook secret hash and
(after the first real payment) "Flutterwave webhook heard". A red cross means act now; read its note.
Then open Plans and tell it what you really pay (Render plan, Supabase plan, Resend plan, how much
OpenAI you are willing to spend, naira per dollar, Flutterwave fee). Pick a plan from each list or
type your own numbers, then Save. Until you do, it uses free-plan defaults and the warnings will
not match your real bill. Press "Send a test alert" and confirm the email reaches you (check spam).

HOW THE LIVE COUNT WORKS
Every open tab pings the server every 30 seconds. Someone counts for 75 seconds after their last
ping. A hidden tab or a phone put down drops off within about a minute. You are not counted (so you
do not inflate it). To test it, open the app in a private window WITHOUT signing in: you appear as
a visitor. Or tap "Count me too" in the Live section.

ABOUT THE COST NUMBERS
They are estimates. OpenAI is counted from tokens at the public price list, so compare with
platform.openai.com now and then. Render, Supabase and Resend use the plans you enter. Flutterwave's
fee is estimated from the rates under Plans (check your own rate in your Flutterwave dashboard).
The "what if" slider assumes about 30 cents of AI per active student a month until you have 10
active students; after that it uses what your real students actually cost.

REFUNDS
Refund in the Flutterwave dashboard. The app does not take the track back automatically on a
Flutterwave refund, so tell the student, and ask me if you want that automated.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
- No green button: the OWNER_EMAILS value must match the email you sign in with exactly (capitals
  do not matter). Render restarts after you change it.
- "A Flutterwave payment could not be confirmed": the student paid but something did not match
  (amount, currency or email). The warning says which. Check the payment in your Flutterwave
  dashboard; if it is real, use "Give a track" in the Control Room.
- "Students cannot pay": the app needs BOTH FLW_PUBLIC_KEY and FLW_SECRET_KEY.
- Payments that are already in your database still count as revenue.
