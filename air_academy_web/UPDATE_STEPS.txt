AIR ACADEMY: STRIPE PAYMENTS + LIVE USERS + CONTROL ROOM UPDATE
===============================================================

WHAT YOU GET
- Stripe checkout instead of Flutterwave (Flutterwave keeps working until you add Stripe keys,
  so no sale is lost while you set Stripe up).
- A green "N live - Control Room" button on YOUR dashboard only. It shows how many people are
  using the app right now (one person = 1, two = 2, and so on), refreshed every few seconds.
- The Control Room (only your email can open it): live users, an OpenAI/Resend/Supabase/Render
  cost meter with warnings and email alerts, this month's bill and projection, a "what if I had
  1,000 students" slider, sales, sign-ups, a launch checklist, and a tool to give a student a track.
- Your students' accounts, courses and payments are NOT touched. New tables are added by
  themselves when the app starts.

STEP 1. COPY THE FILES (PowerShell, inside the folder that has app.py)

    Expand-Archive -Path "$HOME\Downloads\air_academy_stripe_control_room.zip" -DestinationPath . -Force

This adds billing.py, control.py, static/live.js, static/owner.js and replaces app.py, omega.py,
README.md and the static files. It does not touch your .env, your database or your lesson files.

STEP 2. RENDER SETTINGS (Dashboard > your service > Environment)
Add this first. It turns on the live counter and Control Room for you only:

    OWNER_EMAILS = your-own-gmail-address@gmail.com   (the Gmail you sign in with)

Type your real address here on Render only. It is deliberately not in the code or in this file, because your code is on public GitHub.
Also check: APP_ENV=production, and DEMO_UNLOCK must NOT exist.

Settings > Start Command (so one slow OpenAI answer does not freeze everybody else):

    gunicorn app:app --workers 2 --threads 4 --timeout 120

STEP 3. PUSH

    git add .
    git commit -m "Stripe payments, live users and Control Room"
    git push

Render rebuilds. Sign in with your Gmail: you will see the green Control Room button.

STEP 4. OPEN THE CONTROL ROOM > PLANS
Tell it what you really pay (Render plan, Supabase plan, Resend plan, how much OpenAI you are
willing to spend, naira per dollar). Pick a plan from each list or type your own numbers, then Save.
Until you do, it uses free-plan defaults and the warnings will not match your real bill.
Then press "Send a test alert" and confirm the email reaches you (check spam).

STEP 5. STRIPE (test mode first)
READ THIS FIRST: Stripe does not support businesses registered in Nigeria directly. Naira card and
bank-transfer payments are an invite-only Stripe preview, and only for accounts outside Nigeria
(paid out in dollars). Check what your Stripe account allows before you rely on it. Stripe also keeps
a fixed 30 cents per sale, which is a big part of a 4,000 naira sale (about $2.4).

When you are ready:
 1. Stripe dashboard: switch on Test mode. Copy the secret key (sk_test_...).
 2. Render > Environment, add:
        STRIPE_SECRET_KEY = sk_test_...
        STRIPE_CURRENCY   = ngn          (or usd, or whatever your account supports)
        STRIPE_AMOUNT     = 4000         (the price in that currency, e.g. 2.99 for USD)
 3. Stripe > Developers > Webhooks > Add endpoint:
        URL:    https://YOUR-SITE.onrender.com/api/stripe/webhook
        Events: checkout.session.completed
                checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded
                checkout.session.async_payment_failed
                checkout.session.expired
                charge.refunded
                charge.dispute.created
 4. Copy the signing secret (whsec_...) to Render:
        STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET = whsec_...
 5. Test: sign in with a different email, tap Unlock, pay with card 4242 4242 4242 4242 (any future
    date, any CVC). The track should unlock when you come back. In the Control Room > Setup check
    you should see "Stripe webhook heard from Stripe".
 6. Go live: replace the key with sk_live_... and the webhook secret with the live one.
    (The Control Room warns you if a test key is left on a live site.)

Which payment methods appear (cards, bank transfer...) is set in Stripe > Settings > Payment
methods. No code change needed. Refunds are done in the Stripe dashboard: a full refund takes the
track back automatically.

HOW THE LIVE COUNT WORKS
Every open tab pings the server every 30 seconds. Someone counts for 75 seconds after their last
ping. A hidden tab or a phone put down drops off within about a minute. You are not counted (so you
do not inflate it). To test it, open the app in a private window WITHOUT signing in: you appear as
a visitor. Or tap "Count me too" in the Live section.

ABOUT THE COST NUMBERS
They are estimates. OpenAI is counted from tokens at the public price list, so compare with
platform.openai.com now and then. Render, Supabase and Resend use the plans you enter. The
"what if" slider assumes about 30 cents of AI per active student a month until you have 10 active
students; after that it uses what your real students actually cost.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
- No green button: the OWNER_EMAILS value must match the email you sign in with exactly (capitals
  do not matter). Render restarts after you change it.
- "Stripe problem" shown in the Control Room: it shows Stripe's own error message.
- Payments of the old kind (Flutterwave) that are already in your database still count as revenue.
